Operational maturity principles

Practices that apply across all nine domains.

Ownership and accountability
  • Every active record has one directly responsible owner.
  • Escalation includes the decision needed and the time it is needed by.
  • Approval decisions record the approver, timestamp, and reason.
  • Closed work retains evidence and a clear outcome.
Data quality and safety
  • Use dates, amounts, and identifiers in consistent formats.
  • Do not put passwords, tokens, or unnecessary personal data in notes.
  • Preserve history instead of silently overwriting material decisions.
  • Use the smallest practical audience for exports and attachments.
